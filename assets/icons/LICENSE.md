# Icon licences

The apps' icons (`svg/`, generated into every app by `generate.py`) come from:

- **Solar Icons** by 480 Design (R4IN80W), Linear and Bold styles,
  <https://www.figma.com/community/file/1166831539721848736>, licensed
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Attribution is required: every
  app credits it on its licences / acknowledgements screen as
  "Icons: Solar by 480 Design, CC BY 4.0". The glyphs are used as published, except that
  `import_solar.py` rewrites masked strokes as fills and drops no-op transforms so they
  draw on Android.
- **Tabler Icons** (MIT, © Paweł Kuna), <https://tabler.io/icons>: `fingerprint`, `wifi`
  and `wifi-off`, redrawn at Solar's 1.5 line (Solar has no fingerprint or Wi-Fi-off icon).
- **Qasa** (the app's own): `chevron-back` and `chevron-forward`, drawn to the frames of
  the iOS chevrons.
