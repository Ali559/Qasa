Vault list: tighter rows, grouped sections

The Passwords page as it is today, next to two ways of tightening it. In B and C each section (Suggested, Most recent, every letter) is one card and its rows are split by a divider. The tab bar has no grey fill when you press a tab, and site icons always sit as a clean circle. Press a tab in each phone to feel the difference.

Site icons in their circle

Sites ship favicons in every shape. Today each one is shrunk into the middle of a white circle, so square and wide ones look boxed in. The fix happens once, when the icon is saved to the cache: if the icon is solid inside its circle (a square, a rounded square, a circle), it fills the whole circle and is cut to it. If it's solid but not square (a wide logo), the circle takes the logo's own edge colour and the logo fits inside. If it's a glyph on a see-through background, it stays centred on the white circle as today, with its corners softened so a hard square edge never shows.

Sizes: B keeps the 48 tile and brings the row padding from 17 to 12 (row height 72). C goes to 10 and a 44 tile (row height 64). Section labels are 12 semibold with 8 under them and 20 above. Nav buttons on every app: no fill when pressed or hovered, only the colour of the selected tab changes; the desktop sidebar keeps its light accent fill on the selected page.
