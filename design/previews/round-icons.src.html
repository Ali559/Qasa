Phase 3: round icon backgrounds

Every icon that sits on a fill (item tiles, settings badges, Home tiles, the item hero, the bell button) in three shapes, at the Phase 2 sizes. Icons with no fill (tab bar, row buttons) don't change. The back button and add button are already round.

A circle reads as an avatar (a person or an app), which suits the item list and the item hero. Inside rectangular cards and settings rows it sits fine as long as the rows keep their 14 gap; the soft shape (C) is a middle ground that keeps the row rhythm of today with no hard corners.
